# Google Play Store & Online Connectivity Guide (Days 3–30)

Starting with **Day 3**, every app generated by the Daily Android App Factory is designed to be **Privacy-by-Default (Opt-In Online)**, **Android 16 (`targetSdk = 36`) compatible**, and **Google Play Store ready**.

---

## 1. Privacy by Default (Opt-In Only) + Online Resilience
Apps from Day 3 onward follow **zero unsolicited user data** rules:
- **Stateless by Default:** Local history saving (`SharedPreferences`) and online API/webhook calls are **OFF by default** until the user explicitly enables an **Opt-In** toggle (or taps an explicit action button), with a 1-tap **"Wipe All Local Data"** button and zero device IDs or analytics SDKs.
- **Network Permissions:** `android.permission.INTERNET` and `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`.
- **Zero-Bloat Networking:** Uses standard Android/JDK `javax.net.ssl.HttpsURLConnection` inside `kotlinx.coroutines.Dispatchers.IO` (with `10_000 ms` timeouts and `instanceFollowRedirects = false`) and `org.json.JSONObject` / `JSONArray`—keeping Gradle builds clean and fast in Google AI Studio without external HTTP/serialization dependency resolution failures.
- **Free, Keyless Public APIs & Webhooks:** Each day pairs its hardware/DSP core with an optional opt-in live online feature from `30_DAY_SLATE.md` (e.g., Ntfy.sh/Discord webhooks, NOAA SWPC space weather, Open-Meteo, Open-Elevation, OpenFoodFacts, RIPEstat/RDAP, Frankfurter FX, Free Dictionary API, OSM Nominatim).
- **Optional Local Cache:** Only when the user opts in to caching/saving, fetched payloads are stored locally in `MODE_PRIVATE` `SharedPreferences` with timestamps and clear `ONLINE` vs. `OFFLINE` badges.

## 2. Google AI Studio Export & Internal Testing Workflow
Once Google AI Studio (`aistudio.google.com/apps`) finishes compiling an app cleanly:
1. **ZIP Export to PC:** Open the **Download / Export ZIP** option (under the top toolbar / **Settings** or **More** menu) to export the native Gradle + Kotlin + Jetpack Compose project `.zip` to your `Downloads` folder. (`scripts/publish_to_github.ps1 -ExtractOnly` automatically extracts and sanitizes it into `days/day_NN_<slug>/android_project/`.)
2. **Optional Direct Internal Track Publish:** Google AI Studio also provides **Open publish settings** (`Settings -> Publish`) for pushing directly to Google Play Console's Internal Testing track (up to 100 testers).
3. **Play Store Release Kit:** Each day saves `play_store_listing.md` inside `days/day_NN_<slug>/` containing:
   - App Title (<= 30 chars) & Short Description (<= 80 chars)
   - Full Description (<= 4000 chars), Category & Tags
   - Live Privacy Policy URL (`https://github.com/ElizaBackrooms/30-day-android-app-factory/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`)
   - Google Play Data Safety & Permissions Declarations (matching exact opt-in features)

## 3. Packaging & Signing an `.aab` for Google Play Console (2026 Requirements)
Effective **August 31, 2026**, Google Play requires all **new** phone/tablet apps to target **Android 16 (`targetSdk = 36`)** or higher, packaged as a signed **Android App Bundle (`.aab`)** with Play App Signing.

### Step A: Verify Android 16 (`targetSdk = 36`) & Adaptive Compliance
Inside the extracted `android_project/app/build.gradle.kts` and `AndroidManifest.xml`, verify:
- `applicationId = "io.github.elizabackrooms.<app_slug>"` (never `com.example`)
- `compileSdk = 36` and `targetSdk = 36` (bump from `35` if Google AI Studio's cloud template defaulted to `35`)
- `versionCode = 1`, `versionName = "1.0.0"`
- **Edge-to-Edge Mandatory:** `MainActivity` calls `enableEdgeToEdge()` and Compose `Scaffold` applies `innerPadding` / `WindowInsets` cleanly (Android 16 removes the edge-to-edge opt-out).
- **Large-Screen Adaptive Layout:** No `android:screenOrientation` lock or `resizeableActivity="false"` on `<activity>` (ignored on `>= 600dp` screens in Android 16).
- **16 KB Page Size Alignment:** Pure Kotlin + Jetpack Compose apps without native `.so` libraries satisfy Google Play's 16 KB memory page-size requirement automatically.

### Step B: Generate an Upload Keystore (Once per app or shared upload key)
```powershell
keytool -genkey -v -keystore upload-keystore.jks -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias upload
```
*(Keep `upload-keystore.jks` and `keystore.properties` safely outside the repository—`.gitignore` blocks them from GitHub.)*

### Step C: Configure Release Signing & Build `.aab`
Add a `signingConfigs` block in `app/build.gradle.kts` (reading `keystore.properties` outside git) assigned to `buildTypes.release`, then run from the extracted project root:
```powershell
.\gradlew.bat bundleRelease
```
The signed Play Store bundle is output at:
`app\build\outputs\bundle\release\app-release.aab`

### Step D: Upload to Google Play Console
1. Create the app in **Google Play Console** (enroll in **Play App Signing**).
2. Paste metadata from `days/day_NN_<slug>/play_store_listing.md` and set the **Privacy policy URL** to:
   `https://github.com/ElizaBackrooms/30-day-android-app-factory/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`
3. Complete **Data safety** and **Permissions declarations** using `play_store_listing.md`.
4. Upload `app-release.aab` to **Internal testing** first. *(Note: Personal Google Play Developer accounts created on or after November 13, 2023 must run a **Closed testing** track with at least **12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days** before applying for **Production** access.)*
